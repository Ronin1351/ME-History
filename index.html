Method. Classifications are judgment calls compressed to whole years; real transitions are messier. "Imperial province" marks Ottoman-ruled land with no state of its own. Leans before 1917 measure Britain/France versus Russia; the Ottoman Empire in WWI fought both camps and is marked neutral with a Central Powers tag. Yemen is split North/South until unification in 1990. Pakistan before 1947 shows the British Indian provinces that became it; Afghanistan 1879–1919 is marked as a protectorate because Britain ran its foreign policy. Events after mid-2026 (the course of the Iran war, Syria's constitution, Gaza governance) are still moving.
